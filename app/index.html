Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies and no difficulty setting. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter — the game has 15 manual save slots.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, do side quests as they appear 60–80 h

    Finish all 5 chapters and take the optional missions as they pop up. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with the binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist counts progress made during the story even though you cannot see it yet. Do the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) when they are marked; they all stay available after the story.

    Watch out — the only possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning and walks out to the balcony at Jason's house. Follow her, choose Accept, and do the meditation. It is not marked as a normal spot and it is still unclear whether it is required — do it anyway.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist ~100 h

    After the credits the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% of true completion: 100 optional missions, scan 200 of 277 base-model vehicles, study 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all robberies, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations and all 15 target decks. Nothing on it is missable. Finishing it earns A Long Road (gold) and most of the grind trophies along the way.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 h

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands that count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed) and mop up whatever is left on the checklist. The platinum pops the moment the last of the other 54 is earned.

Cheats disable trophies and saving — and they only unlock after the story anyway, so keep them off for the whole run. Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; PSNProfiles / Exophase — official trophy list.

No trophies match this filter. There are no online trophies — GTA VI launches as a single-player game with no new GTA Online, so every one of the 55 is earned offline.
